1. Glance Board  [glance-board]  <- take this one unless it clashes with the brief
   Governing asset    one hero readout line (a bold value with a smaller same-baseline unit) that repeats on the widget and the Live Activity
   Display treatment  bold values with smaller same-baseline unit suffixes
   Hero moment 1      the tracked entity changes state (the label and its semantic colour update together)
   Hero moment 2      scrubbing the curve with a selection tick per data point
   Shell              quiet: neutral chrome, status colour is the only hue
   Mode / density     light / high
   Composition        user-composed with a default order
   Depth              one depth cue on the governing asset only, dropped under Reduce Motion (text-sourced: stills cannot show it)
   Posture            native iOS: system bars, sheets, gestures and glass come from the platform
   Corpus warning     Warning, not a gate: flag a screen that shows more than one competing entity per line or sheet, status colour used decoratively, or brand hue leaking into status.
